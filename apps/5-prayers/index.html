Your data, explained

No mystery.
Just the details.

Prayer preferences and saved locations are primarily stored on your device. Location and sensor data power the features you choose. There are no in-app ads or dedicated analytics SDKs in the current release.

Good to know

A few helpful answers.

Do I have to allow location access?+

No. You can deny device location permission and use manual location search instead. Search and geocoding lookups may be processed by the platform's location services.

Can I customize the prayer times?+

Yes. The app supports calculation-method choices, Asr and high-latitude settings, prayer adjustments, Hijri adjustments, and related display preferences.

Are there ads or an account?+

There are no in-app ads and no account is required. Optional one-time support purchases are processed by Google Play or Apple.

Can I share a monthly timetable?+

Yes. The app generates a PDF on your device. If you open or share it, the receiving app or service handles that copy under its own privacy practices.
