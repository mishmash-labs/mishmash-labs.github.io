Good to know

A few helpful answers.

Which puzzle modes are included?+

Word Bridge, Logic Deduction, Letter Lattice, Fragment Forge, Cipher Lock, Category Matrix, Lexical Extraction, and String Resolution.

Is my progress saved?+

Yes. Puzzle progress, statistics, achievements, and preferences are stored on your device. Clearing app data or uninstalling may remove that local progress, subject to platform backup behavior.

What does Pro add?+

Pro removes ads and unlocks additional benefits, including saved hints, challenge swaps, and cabinet customization. The app shows the current purchase details.

Does the app use analytics?+

The current iOS version uses Firebase Analytics to measure app usage and campaign installs. The free version also uses Google advertising services. The privacy policy explains these services and your choices.
