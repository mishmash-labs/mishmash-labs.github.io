Your data, explained

No mystery.
Just the details.

Favorites, itineraries, and visit history are stored locally, with no GPS permission. Google services handle ads, consent and font downloads. The app can fetch guide updates from GitHub Pages and Android app updates through Google Play. App stores handle optional reviews.

Good to know

A few helpful answers.

Do I need an account or GPS access?+

No. An account is not required, and Explore Lahore does not request runtime device-location permission. Map links open a third-party service.

Can I use the guide offline?+

Core place data ships with the app and your saved plans are local. Ads, font downloads, external websites, maps, and automatic guide-data refreshes need a connection.

Are there ads?+

Yes. The app uses Google Mobile Ads in selected areas and Google's consent flow where required. Available privacy choices can be reviewed in the app.

Can I share my itinerary?+

Yes. The app passes your selected itinerary to the system share sheet, where you choose the receiving app or person.
