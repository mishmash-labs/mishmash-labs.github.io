Your data, explained

No mystery.
Just the details.

FPL sign-in connects directly to Premier League services; public Team ID viewing needs no password. Saved credentials use secure storage. Google handles ads and consent. RevenueCat manages purchases and receives the active FPL entry ID. Widgets use public team data; there is no Firebase Analytics integration.

Good to know

A few helpful answers.

Do I need to sign in to Fantasy Premier League?+

You can view public information using a Team ID or Explore without entering a password. Sign in with an existing FPL account for private information, Pick Team and Transfers. An internet connection is needed; proFPL does not create or operate your FPL account.

Is this an official Premier League app?+

No. proFPL is an independent companion and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or associated with the Premier League or Fantasy Premier League.

What is proFPL Plus?+

Plus is an optional upgrade with monthly and yearly subscriptions and a lifetime purchase option. A valid Plus entitlement suppresses ads. Available features and localized prices are shown in the app.

How are saved accounts handled?+

If you choose to save an account, credentials and reusable session information use your device's platform secure storage. You can remove saved accounts in Settings. Never send passwords or authentication tokens in a support message.
