Good to know

A few helpful answers.

Does it work offline?+

Yes. Core unit conversions use locally bundled conversion data and work without an internet connection.

What is the difference between Konvertr and Pro?+

Konvertr is the free, focused unit converter. Konvertr Pro is a separate paid app with a calculator, favorites, conversion and calculator history, themes, multi-view workflows, and 19 utility tools.

Do I need an account?+

No. Konvertr does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs in the reviewed release.
