A closer look

Small details.
A useful difference.

Here's what you can do with Autofolio.

01

A log for every powertrain

Record refuels and EV charges with quantities, prices, odometer readings, and notes. Follow consumption and cost per kilometre or mile.

02

Maintenance, without the guesswork

Keep services, repairs, tires, and recurring bills together. Set reminders by date, odometer, or whichever comes first. Optional notifications are date-based; mileage limits are checked in the app.

03

Documents in their place

Attach photos or PDFs for insurance, registration, inspections, and warranties. Track expiry dates alongside your other reminders.

04

Mileage you control

Manually log business and personal trips using start and end odometer readings. No GPS recording, background location tracking, or automatic tax-deduction calculation.

05

Receipts to records

Scan Latin-script receipts on-device to pre-fill a fuel or charging entry, then review before saving. Import CSV records and export CSV or JSON.

06

Your garage, backed up

Create local backups with attached photos and documents. Pro adds PDF reports and vehicle passports. Backups are not encrypted, so protect files you save or share.

Your data, explained

No mystery.
Just the details.

Vehicle records and supported imports are processed on your device. There are no ads, behavioral analytics, or location tracking. Backups are not encrypted. Stores handle purchases, reviews and Android updates; files you share are handled by the recipients you choose.

Good to know

A few helpful answers.

What is included for free?+

Free mode includes two vehicles, including inactive ones, and five lifetime saved receipt scans. The optional lifetime Pro purchase adds unlimited vehicles and receipt scans, PDF reports, vehicle passports, and alternative themes.

Does Autofolio sync across devices?+

Automatic cross-device sync is not available. Core tracking works offline, and you can create and restore local backups. Backup files are not encrypted.

Is Pro a subscription?+

No. Lifetime Pro is a one-time in-app purchase where available. Current pricing is shown in the app and may vary by region.

Which languages are supported?+

English, German, French, Spanish, Hindi, Urdu, and Arabic, with right-to-left support for Urdu and Arabic.
