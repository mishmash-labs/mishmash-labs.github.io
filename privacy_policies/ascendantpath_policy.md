# Privacy Policy

**Progress Knight: Ascendant Path (Ascendant Path)**

**Publisher: Mishmash Labs**

**Last updated: November 6, 2026**

Mishmash Labs ("we", "us", or "our") is responsible for the information handling
described in this policy. This policy covers the Ascendant Path mobile game,
also referred to as Progress Knight: Ascendant Path, on Android and iOS.
Only the store title fields omit the colon to meet their character limits.

You can play without creating a game account. Your journey is saved on your
device. However, the game is not a "no data collected" app: advertising,

For privacy questions or requests, contact **mishmash.labs@gmail.com** with the
subject **Ascendant Path privacy**.

## 1. Information stored on your device

The game stores your progress, resources, achievements, story choices,
preferences, and save times locally. It also keeps backup or recovery copies
to help restore a journey if a save becomes unreadable or is replaced.

To measure time away fairly, the game stores elapsed-time information,
including phone uptime, a restart-session identifier, and, when available, a
verified online timestamp. The restart-session identifier is used to recognize
whether the phone has restarted; it is not used by us as an advertising
identifier.

Your game save and these local clock records are not automatically uploaded to
a Mishmash Labs game server. Your operating system or device backup service
may separately back up application data according to your device and account
settings.

### Backups and the clipboard

When you choose to copy a backup or recovery data, the game places that text on
your device's clipboard. When you import a journey, it reads the text you enter.
Importing replaces your current progress and may retain the previous journey
for recovery.

Backup text includes game progress and preferences. It is not a password or a
securely encrypted vault. Other applications, clipboard synchronization, or
services you choose to paste it into may gain access under their own rules.
Keep backups somewhere you trust. We receive a backup only if you choose to
send it to us, such as with a support request.

The free game uses **Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads** for banner ads and
optional rewarded ads. An ad request can send information to Google and the
advertising services involved in serving or measuring that ad.

Depending on the platform, permissions, ad configuration, and applicable
consent choices, this can include:

- IP address and approximate location inferred from it.
- Advertising identifiers and other app- or device-related identifiers.
- Device, operating-system, and application information.
- Ad impressions, taps, video views, and other ad or app interactions.
- Crash, performance, and other diagnostic information.

This information can be used to serve and measure ads, analyze advertising
performance, maintain service quality, and detect fraud or abuse. Where
permitted and enabled, it may also be used for personalized advertising or
cross-app advertising measurement. Non-personalized or limited ads can still
involve data processing.

The game does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or
precise GPS location for gameplay.

Rewarded ads open only when you choose the ad offer. Banners do not require
choosing a rewarded ad. Fortune's Favor removes the game's ads while that
purchase is recognized. It does not erase information already processed by
advertising providers, remove purchase-service processing, or prevent online
time checks.

## 3. Purchases and purchase restoration

**Fortune's Favor** is an optional one-time purchase, not a subscription.
Payments are handled by **Apple's App Store** or **Google Play**. We do not
receive your full payment-card details.

The game uses **RevenueCat** to check purchases, grant access, and restore
ownership. When configured, this service may be contacted even if you have not
bought anything, so the game can check whether you already own the favor.

Purchase processing can include an automatically generated anonymous app-user
identifier, receipt or purchase-token information, transaction and product
identifiers, purchase history and status, store and country/currency
information, and technical information needed to communicate with the service.
"Anonymous" means that no named game account is required; it does not mean that
the purchase service processes no data.

We do not ask you to provide a name or email address to RevenueCat through a
game account, and the game does not add advertising identifiers to RevenueCat
for advertising attribution.

Purchase records are separate from journey backups. Starting a new story,
importing a save, or deleting a local save does not delete store transactions
or RevenueCat purchase records. Restoring purchases normally requires the same
store account. Cross-store Android/iOS purchase sharing is not promised.

Provider information:

- RevenueCat: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/
- Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
- Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 4. Online time checks

At startup and when you return to the game, it may contact
`https://www.google.com/generate_204` to read the response's date and help
verify elapsed time. The request includes a random value to avoid a cached
response. It does not include your game save, purchase identifier, or
restart-session identifier.

As with an ordinary internet connection, Google receives the source IP address
and connection/request information and may process them under its privacy
policy. This request is for time verification, not a separate analytics
service.

Core play can continue without internet. The game uses phone uptime where
possible; some time away after a phone restart cannot be verified offline and
may not earn offline progress.

## 8. Retention and deletion

Local progress and recovery copies remain until overwritten or removed from
the device. **Begin a new story** replaces the active journey but can preserve
the earlier journey as recovery data; it is not a complete data-erasure tool.

On Android, clearing the app's storage removes its local data. On iOS, deleting
the app rather than offloading it generally removes local app data. Device
backup services may retain or restore copies separately. Delete exported
backups and manage operating-system backups yourself if you want those copies
removed.

Support information is retained for as long as reasonably needed to resolve
the request, maintain relevant records, or comply with legal requirements.
Purchase records may need to remain available for restoration, fraud
prevention, accounting, or legal obligations. Advertising providers, billing
platforms, and other independent providers apply their own retention rules.
Uninstalling the app does not automatically erase their records.

Contact us to request deletion of information we control. We will explain any
applicable legal retention requirement or limitation. Deleting records needed
to recognize a purchase may affect restoration; we will explain this where
relevant before acting on a request.
